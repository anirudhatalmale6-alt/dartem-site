Managed IT support

Responsive day-to-day support for your people and systems, so technology problems do not hold up your work.

When something stops working, you need someone who knows your systems and answers quickly. Our managed support gives your team a single point of contact for everyday IT issues, maintenance and updates.

What is included

  • Helpdesk support for your staff
  • Proactive patching, updates and maintenance
  • User setup, devices and licence management
  • Regular reporting and review meetings

How we work with you

We onboard your systems, document them and agree response times with you. From then on, your team contacts us directly and we keep you informed of what has been done.
